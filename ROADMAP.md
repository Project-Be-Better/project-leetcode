## 📜 Methodology: "Recognize → Approach → Code"

1. **Pattern Recognition (30s):** Why is this X pattern?
2. **Approach (2m):** Mental model / Analogy.
3. **Edge Cases (1m):** Empty, single, duplicates, limits.
4. **Code (5-10m):** Idiomatic Python.
5. **Complexity:** Time & Space analysis.
